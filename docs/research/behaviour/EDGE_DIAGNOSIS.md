Evidence audited:
- the code in `research/behaviour/` at commit `c8b6195`;
- the committed tables in `research/behaviour/results/run-38279444111/` (discovery run 38279444111, post-hoc run 38288465542);
- `AUDIT.md`, `FINDINGS.md` and `tests/test_behaviour.py`.

Line numbers refer to `c8b6195`. Each item is labelled **VERIFIED** (checked in code or reproduced from the tables), **HYPOTHESIS** (a plausible explanation, not yet tested) or **UNRESOLVED** (cannot be settled with the evidence kept).

## A. Verified facts

1. **Count of tests (VERIFIED).**
   - 498,252 = hypothesis masks × 4 horizons, generated by `engine.py:218` `hypotheses()` and kept when they fire on at least 30 sessions (`engine.py:285`). That is about 124,600 masks over 102 instruments.
   - 381,580 met the discovery minimum (n ≥ 40 trades and ≥ 20 weeks).
   - The masks are heavily nested: every `EVENT | condition` sits inside `EVENT | all`, DOWSLOT sits inside SLOT, and the 3-candle sequences partition the CANDLE family.
   - So 498,252 is a count of tests, not of independent ideas.
2. **The recurrence counts reproduce (VERIFIED).**
   - `recurring_catalogue.csv` has 34 rows; 23 have `persists_in_holdout = True` (rule at `engine.py:379-380`).
   - They contain only **29 distinct (stock, hypothesis) pairs**, because horizons are counted separately.
   - They contain only **33 distinct statistics**, because aliases repeat: AUROPHARMA `rvol_thrust_up | all` ≡ `rvol_thrust_up | rvol=high`, since the event already requires relative volume ≥ 2.5.
   - They contain only **27 distinct (stock, base event or state)** behaviours.
3. **Profitability funnel (VERIFIED from `profit_catalogue.csv` and `cost_sensitivity.json`).**
   - Base cost: 3 discovery candidates, 0 pass validation.
   - Low cost: 62 candidates, 0 pass validation.
   - High cost: 0 candidates.
   - Zero cost (diagnostic only): 40 pass validation, mostly variants of "short LICI to 15:15".
4. **The walk-forward result is one cluster (VERIFIED, `walk_forward_breakdown.json`).**
   - The last window (Jul–Oct 2026) has 2,075 of 2,410 trades, from 65 rules on **ANANDRATHI only**, across 16 weeks.
   - Every other window combined: 335 trades, net (base) +0.038% per trade, t = 0.38.
5. **Features are causal (VERIFIED).**
   - Rolling medians use previous sessions only (`features.py:54`), as do beta (`features.py:157`) and the NIFTY regime (`features.py:169`).
   - Entries are at the next bar's open (`features.py:114`); exits at a fixed bar's close (`features.py:135`).
   - Test: `test_states_and_events_never_use_future_bars`.
6. **Chronology of selection (VERIFIED).**
   - The trade direction is chosen on discovery only (`engine.py` `evaluate`, `side`).
   - Validation is a separate gate (`engine.py:542` `finalize`).
   - The walk-forward trains only on the 18 months before each test quarter (`engine.py:418-432`).
7. **The holdout year is contaminated (VERIFIED).**
   - Holdout statistics were computed for every test (`engine.py:349-362`) and published in the `all_tests` artifact.
   - `posthoc.py:192` selected "promising hypotheses" using `persists_in_holdout`, and `FINDINGS.md` §4 reported their holdout returns. That is selection on the test set.
   - In addition, all of Oct 2022 – Oct 2026 has now been inspected.
   - **No new hypothesis can treat any part of 2022-10-13 .. 2026-10-12 as an untouched test.**
8. **Inference (VERIFIED).**
   - Standard errors are clustered by calendar week per hypothesis (`stats.py:17`).
   - Benjamini–Hochberg is applied per stock and globally (`stats.py:50`).
   - Holdout confidence intervals use a 4-week moving-block bootstrap (`stats.py:87`).
   - One trade per hypothesis per session, so a hypothesis's trades never overlap.

## B. Methodological defects (VERIFIED in code)

1. **No stop-loss execution.** Every exit is time-based. Favourable/adverse excursions are reported but never act on the trade (`features.py:113-137`).
2. **Optimistic fills.** Entry is at the next bar's open with zero latency (`features.py:114`). Slippage is folded into "fees" and not modelled separately (`engine.py:56`).
3. **Spread estimate.** A 1-minute Corwin–Schultz estimate (mixes volatility into the spread), capped at 0.10% (`engine.py:95-116`); 2 stocks hit the cap. Option costs were never modelled.
4. **The "excess over baseline" is not a tradable signal.** Its baseline is each period's own average, including later days of that period (`engine.py:179-185`, `EB` at `engine.py:296`). Fine for description only.
5. **The structure claim in FINDINGS §1 is not valid evidence.** It compared 4,241 results with gross t ≥ 3 against about 1,030 expected (`posthoc.py:107-109`):
   - the expectation assumes independent tests;
   - gross t includes each stock's drift.
6. **Corporate actions were never checked.** Previous-day high/low (`features.py:289`), gap (`features.py:300`), daily return (`features.py:138`) and beta (`features.py:157`) would all be distorted around unadjusted ex-dates.
7. **Survivorship and selection.** The universe is today's top-99 by traded value (`deploy/live-core/behaviour_fetch.py:31`), which is information from the end of the sample.
8. **Session filter.** Sessions under 95% complete are dropped (`data.py:75`). The next gap is then measured from the previous *kept* session. Affects at most about 4 sessions per instrument.

## C. Hypotheses (explanations to test, not conclusions)

1. **H-drift:** most "profitable" gross results are stock-level drift lasting several months (LICI down, ANANDRATHI down, KALYANKJIL up), not intraday conditional behaviour. Pre-registered as H5.
2. **H-cost:** the recurring stock-specific behaviours are real but about 0.05–0.15% per trade, the same order as a realistic round trip (0.08–0.17%).
3. **H-exec:** a structural stop, a structural target and a decision on a cleaner event may lift per-trade expectancy above costs where time-only exits could not. Pre-registered as H1–H4 and H6.

## D. Unresolved uncertainties

1. **Whether Dhan's 1-minute history is split- and bonus-adjusted.** The BSE 2:1 bonus (2025) and the TMPV demerger and symbol change (TATAMOTORS → TMPV, Oct 2025) are the known exposures. No raw data was kept, so they can only be checked on a new fetch. Pre-registered handling: `integrity.py`.
2. **How large the survivorship bias is.** It cannot be measured without a point-in-time F&O list, which we don't have.
3. **Whether 1-minute timestamps mark bar starts.** All 975 sessions contain the 09:15 slot, which is consistent with bar starts, but no external reference was checked.
4. **Whether the cross-trade correlation (ρ) assumed in the power analysis is realistic.** It will be measured on the already-seen in-sample period before the confirmation is evaluated. That does not change the decision rules.

## E. What the search establishes, and what it does not

- **Establishes:**
  - Stable, stock-specific intraday behaviour exists in a minority of names (27 distinct behaviours in 13 of 102).
  - None produced a validated net edge under the tested costs and time-only exits.
  - The walk-forward's positive result is one single-stock drift episode.
- **Does not establish:**
  - that no executable edge exists with stops, targets and better event definitions;
  - that the costs used are accurate;
  - that the results are free of corporate-action distortion;
  - anything about options P&L.

## F. Ranked next research questions

1. Do any of the six pre-registered mechanisms survive realistic execution (1-minute stops, latency, itemised costs) on genuinely untouched data? (`PREREGISTRATION.md`)
2. Is the multi-month drift effect (H5) real once survivorship is removed? Only forward data can answer this.
3. Are Dhan's intraday candles adjusted for corporate actions? This must be answered before any previous-day-level feature is trusted.
4. What are the actual realised spread and slippage for these stocks in the chosen execution window? This needs live order-book snapshots, not range estimators.
5. For anything confirmed: does the underlying edge survive in ATM stock options (premium decay, IV, option spreads)? Requires the expired-options data, under separate approval.
6. Measure, rather than assume, the dependence between trades on the same day (ρ). This decides how much pooling can buy.
